I'm an engineering leader who combines technical depth, people development, and organisational judgment. I build teams and platforms, help engineers take ownership, and connect engineering decisions to the needs of the business.

My work spans architecture reviews, stakeholder relationships, delivery planning, and direct implementation. I adjust my involvement to the situation: giving experienced engineers room to lead, challenging assumptions, removing obstacles, and contributing code where it helps the team deliver.

I grew up in Xanthi, a provincial, multi-faith border city in northern Greece, where cultural intelligence was part of daily life. I built a path through websites, 40+ freelance projects, a Linux user group, and a national open-source conference before moving to Leeds in 2015. Across financial services, regulated utilities, global retail, and public sector technology, the thread has been building what was missing: a community, a platform, a team, or a way of working.

<p class="pull-quote">Everywhere I've worked, I've built the thing that wasn't there.</p>

I've been mentoring engineers since before it was part of any job title - from teaching adults to use computers in Greek classrooms to developing engineers who've gone on to lead teams of their own.

## Currently
{: .icon-briefcase}

**Software Engineering Manager at Utility Warehouse** (FTSE 250) - Leading a specialist team responsible for six platform services serving <span class="stat">15+</span> engineering teams and <span class="stat">8</span> content teams.

Led the engineering delivery of UW's main website rebrand alongside the transition from CMSv2 to CMSv3 (Oct 2025 - Apr 2026). Working with two engineers and additional temporary support, combined platform modernisation, simpler content components, and content migration with a coordinated launch across a <span class="stat">522-page</span> estate. Guided technical decisions, negotiated scope and capacity, contributed redesigned components, and coordinated stakeholders through launch. [Read how we delivered it →](/leadership/2026/11/11/beyond-the-rebrand-leading-the-cmsv3-migration.html)

I'm undertaking the [Oxford Emerging Leaders Programme (Blended)](https://www.sbs.ox.ac.uk/programmes/online-learning/oxford-emerging-leaders-programme) at **Saïd Business School, University of Oxford**, in the first cohort that started in September 2026. This executive education combines live online learning with a subsequent Oxford immersion. I'm developing a leadership playbook around a live organisational challenge, with a focus on systems thinking, influence across departments, and strategic execution. [More about my executive development →](/credentials/#executive-education)

## What I Do
{: .icon-compass}

<div class="leadership-card" markdown="1">
**Technical Direction & Engineering**: I review PRs daily, discuss technical direction with my engineers, and scrutinise architecture while giving them ownership of solutions. I also implement software: redesigned components for the rebrand, and an ongoing project to move our link shortener into an independent application. That project combines learning, architecture, and execution outside the team's critical path.
</div>

<div class="leadership-card" markdown="1">
**People & Team Development**: I lead cross-cultural teams with clear ownership and room to grow. I've driven **3 promotions** through evidence-based cases and mentored engineers who now lead teams themselves. Across three restructurings, my teams have had **zero voluntary departures**. I use delivery data and team engagement surveys to inform conversations, workload decisions, and career development.
</div>

<div class="leadership-card" markdown="1">
**Delivery & Organisational Influence**: I connect quarterly planning to the capacity and dependencies behind it, raise risks early, and work across departments to resolve them. For the rebrand, I made the website's role in the company-wide launch explicit, secured temporary engineering support, and helped defer tracking and experimentation work to protect the deadline.
</div>

<div class="leadership-card" markdown="1">
**Product & Commercial Judgment**: I connect platform investment to the people who use it and the business it supports. For CMSv3, that meant fewer components, feedback from content editors, and less friction in their daily work; we observed fewer support requests after launch. I also manage a **six-figure vendor portfolio**, contribute to CapEx/OpEx planning, and evaluate vendors against technical, user, and commercial needs.
</div>
